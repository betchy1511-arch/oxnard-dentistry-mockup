Know the signs

Do I Have Periodontal Disease?

01

What Is Periodontal Disease?

Periodontal disease, also known as gum disease, is a bacterial infection of the gums that can lead to inflammation, gum recession, and, if untreated, tooth loss.

02

Early Symptoms

Sensitive gums, red or pink gums, slightly loose teeth, and pink-tinged saliva.

03

Advanced Symptoms

Pain while brushing or touching gums, bleeding while brushing, noticeably loose teeth, and gaps forming between teeth and gums.

04

Linked Health Conditions

It can also contribute to diabetes, heart disease, kidney disease, and Alzheimer's disease.

Treatment

How Is Periodontal Disease Treated?

  1. 1

    Early Stages

    A professional cleaning and improved at-home oral hygiene, along with a prescribed mouth rinse, may be sufficient.

  2. 2

    Advanced Stages

    Scaling and root planing may be necessary to address deeper issues.

  3. 3

    Scaling

    Scaling involves removing plaque, tartar, and bacteria from the teeth and root surfaces using specialized dental tools.

  4. 4

    Root Planing

    Root planing smooths the cleaned root surfaces, making it more difficult for bacteria and plaque to reattach. This helps your gums heal and provides stronger support for your teeth.
