He offers a full range of general, restorative and cosmetic treatment for patients from youngsters to seniors. At your visit, expect a comprehensive and gentle exam.

Before you book

Questions Patients Ask Us

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. Book online, or call our office to request an appointment.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept Denti-Cal, most PPO plans, and HMOs. If your plan is not listed, please call our office to confirm coverage.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us as soon as you can. We accept same-day appointments for patients with dental emergencies.

Do you offer financing?

We work with multiple financing companies. See our financing page or ask us at your visit.

Where are you and when are you open?

We are at 1730 East Gonzales Rd., Oxnard, CA 93036. We are open Monday to Thursday 9:00 am to 5:00 pm and Friday 9:00 am to 4:00 pm.
