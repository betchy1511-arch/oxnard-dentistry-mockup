Before you book

Questions Patients Ask Us

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. Book the first open time online, or call us and we will find a time that suits you.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept Denti-Cal, most PPO plans and HMOs. Call us with your plan details and we will confirm your coverage.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us as soon as you can. We offer same-day appointments for patients with dental emergencies.

Can I pay for treatment over time?

Yes. We work with several financing companies. Ask us at your visit or see our financing page.

Where are you and when are you open?

We are at 1730 East Gonzales Rd., Oxnard, CA 93036. We are open Monday to Thursday 9:00 am to 5:00 pm and Friday 9:00 am to 4:00 pm.
